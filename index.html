Privacy Policy

All In One Device Manager — clear information about device access, local processing, advertising and your privacy.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the All In One Device Manager Android app ("the app") handles information when you use it. The app is published by WildCat Developers BD.

1. Information the app accesses

To provide its device-management features, the app may access or read information available through Android, including:

  • CPU usage and system memory (RAM) statistics;
  • device model, manufacturer, brand, Android version and other hardware characteristics;
  • available storage, display, camera and sensor capabilities;
  • the list and basic details of installed applications, where Android permits access; and
  • sensor readings or hardware information when you open the relevant feature.

This information is used to display device status, hardware details, usage graphs and installed-app management features. App-management actions such as opening an app's system uninstall flow are initiated by you.

2. Local processing and developer collection

Device information used for monitoring and hardware screens is processed on your device to provide the app's features. The publisher does not operate an account system or its own server for collecting CPU, RAM, hardware, sensor or installed-app information through the app. The publisher does not sell this locally accessed device information.

Some information may be retained locally by Android or the app for normal operation and preferences. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private local data, subject to Android and device backup behavior.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including interstitial advertising. Google and its advertising technology providers may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, advertising or app-set identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud-prevention purposes. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location.

Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit (TLS). You can learn more here:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google Play
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings

4. Sharing of information

The publisher does not share locally processed CPU, RAM, hardware, sensor or installed-app information with its own servers. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads may be shared by Google with its service providers, advertising partners or other parties as described in Google's applicable privacy terms and disclosures.

5. Permissions and your choices

Android controls the app's access to device features and installed-app information. You can manage available app permissions in Android Settings. Some features may be limited if access is denied or restricted by your Android version or device manufacturer. You can also control or reset your Android advertising ID through your device's settings, where available.

6. Data security and retention

Device readings used by the app are processed locally. The publisher does not maintain a separate server-side database of these readings. Google Mobile Ads handles advertising-related information under Google's security, retention and privacy practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose device utility and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the publisher, contact the developer using the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning the app, contact the developer using the developer contact email displayed on the All In One Device Manager Google Play Store listing.

Important: Google Mobile Ads data practices may change when the SDK is updated. The publisher will review this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declarations when SDKs or app behavior change.
